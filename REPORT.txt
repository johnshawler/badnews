PORT REPORT
Game:                  Bad News Baseball (Tecmo 1990, NES, USA ROM, MMC1 128 KB PRG + 128 KB CHR, 60 Hz)
Class and route:       Assembly or disassembly for a Z80, 6502 or 6809 machine (here a 6502 binary, ported at the user's
                       request): traced in a reference NES (tools/nesref), translated once on the host (tools/recomp.py)
                       to C for the host test and to 68020 assembly for the Amiga; PPU, mapper, controller and APU replaced
                       by plain-C state (src/nes.c, 68k fast paths in src/nes68k.s), an AGA display (src/video.c) and
                       Paula sound (src/sound.c). Started with tools/new_port.sh from the Little League Baseball port.
Display:               planes=4 scroll=1 fetch=0 buffers=1 sprites used=7 (3 attached 64-pixel pairs + sprite 6, reused
                       down the screen) colours=32 (16 background + 16 sprite = the NES's 25 on screen)
Busiest moment:        field play: camera scrolling in x and y, 20-40 8x16 NES sprites, crowd animated by CHR-bank switching
                       = not a Step 2 sum (translated logic dominates); measured with 4 MB fast RAM: logic 15-25k colour
                       clocks a tick (1.2 ticks a frame), display 20-45k a frame (FEASIBILITY.md, tools/late.py)
Cut from the original: sprite-behind-background priority (sprites always in front); sprite clusters are dropped when more
                       than four overlap vertically (title logo, one batter's bat between innings); a scroll change at a
                       raster split is shown in whole tiles (the home-run screen's pan moves in 8-pixel steps) and the
                       intro's vertical split ($2006) is not shown; the crowd animation reaches the screen a few frames late;
                       screen loads and camera cuts show black frames while the screen is drawn; the 32-pixel side borders
                       show the backdrop colour; very high NES notes are clamped to Paula's fastest rate; DMC samples play
                       at half their rate (16.5 kHz) on channel 3 in place of the noise while they last.
Stock A1200 bench:     frames=1915 late=606 worst_cck=1907235 avg_cck=67964 budget_cck=71051 worst_pct=2684 avg_pct=95 display_hz=50 shown_fps=37.9
                       ticks=3000 checksum=b9481e88  (FS-UAE 3.1.66, tools/port.sh, AROS ROM)
With 4 MB fast RAM:    frames=2422 late=85 worst_cck=1057530 avg_cck=37932 budget_cck=71051 worst_pct=1488 avg_pct=53 display_hz=50 shown_fps=48.3
                       ticks=3000 checksum=b9481e88  (tools/port.sh fast)
Host checksum:         b9481e88 after 3000 ticks  equal
Frame rate claim:      48.3 fps shown with 4 MB fast RAM, 37.9 fps on the stock A1200, logic at 60 ticks a second (game time
                       follows the clock). 50 Hz NOT held on either: tools/port.sh prints FAIL on both. Where the late
                       vertical blanks come from, measured in a debug build of the same bench with fast RAM (tools/debug_run.sh
                       + tools/late.py; 102 late there, the profiler costs a little): 15 the game's one-time start-up (one
                       tick of ~1.1 million colour clocks), 42 black frames while a new screen is built (two 128-write slices
                       of the load code in one frame), 45 play frames that need two ticks and land a few thousand colour
                       clocks over the frame.
                       The day-one load measurement predicted that the stock machine cannot hold 50 Hz in field play.
                       Cheapest upgrade likely to reach 50 Hz in play: a 68030 accelerator with fast RAM (not measured).
                       Emulator results are an upper bound: the final judge is "bnb bench 3000" on a real A1200 with fast RAM.
Not done, not tested:  real A1200 hardware; NTSC machines; Kickstart 3.1 (the user's ROM ran an earlier build of the bench with
                       an equal checksum, this build was not re-run on it); the sound by ear (checked as a WAV from
                       tools/shost.c); interactive play by a person (1 player mode is translated from traces with random
                       input, never played); the intro's split; a 68030 bench; CI (the ROM cannot be committed to this public
                       repository, so CI has nothing to build).
How to run:            put the ROM at original/bnb.nes, run tools/make_assets.sh, then tools/port.sh (or tools/build.sh with
                       the SOURCES line of port.conf). Copy dist/bnb to the Amiga and run "bnb" from a Shell ("bnb bench
                       3000" for the proof run). Joystick in port 2: stick = pad, fire = A, second fire = B. Keys: cursor
                       keys = pad, X = A, Z or Alt = B, Space = Select, Return/Enter = Start, Esc = quit.
