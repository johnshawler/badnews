PORT REPORT
Game:                  Bad News Baseball (Tecmo 1990, NES, USA ROM, MMC1 128 KB PRG + 128 KB CHR, 60 Hz)
Class and route:       Binary 6502 code -> "Assembly or disassembly for a Z80, 6502 or 6809 machine": translated once on
                       the host (tools/recomp.py) to C (host test) and to 68020 assembly (Amiga), entry points found by
                       tracing the ROM in a reference NES (tools/nesref) and in the frame model (tools/nesref/verify.c
                       VERIFY_COV). PPU, mapper, controller and APU replaced by plain-C state (src/nes.c, with 68k fast
                       paths in src/nes68k.s), an AGA display (src/video.c) and Paula sound (src/sound.c). Started with
                       tools/new_port.sh from the Little League Baseball port. The user asked for this ROM to be ported.
Display:               planes=4 scroll=1 fetch=0 buffers=1 sprites used=7 (3 attached 64-pixel pairs + sprite 6, reused
                       down the screen) colours=32 (16 background + 16 sprite = the NES's 25 on screen)
Busiest moment:        field play: camera scrolling in x and y, ~20-40 8x16 NES sprites, crowd animated by CHR-bank
                       switching, NES CPU 70-90% busy (6,600-8,300 6502 instructions a frame). Not a Step 2 sum: logic
                       alone is ~45k colour clocks a tick stock and ~17k with fast RAM (x1.2 ticks per frame), display
                       ~24k a frame (debug build, tools/debug_run.sh + tools/frames.py).
Cut from the original: sprite-behind-background priority (sprites always in front); sprite clusters are dropped when more
                       than four overlap vertically (seen on the title logo and one batter between innings); a scroll
                       change at a raster split is shown in whole tiles (the home-run screen's pan moves in 8-pixel
                       steps) and the intro's vertical split ($2006) is not shown; the crowd animation reaches the
                       screen a few frames late; the 32-pixel side borders show the backdrop colour; screen loads and
                       some camera cuts are shown after a few black or frozen frames; very high NES notes are clamped to
                       Paula's fastest rate; DMC samples are played at half their rate (16.5 kHz) on channel 3 in place
                       of the noise while they last.

Frame rate claim:      33.9 fps shown on the stock A1200, 43.4 fps with 4 MB fast RAM, logic at 60 ticks a second (game
                       time follows the clock). 50 Hz NOT held: tools/port.sh prints FAIL on both. The day-one load
                       measurement predicted this: field play needs ~80-130k colour clocks of logic per frame on the
                       stock machine. Menus and quiet field play run at 50 Hz on fast RAM; the late frames there are
                       screen loads (one tick of up to 65,000 6502 instructions), camera cuts and frames just over 71k.
                       Hand-written replacements so far: the MMC1 bank switch, the controller read, the metasprite
                       builder, PPU and APU register writes, OAM DMA, nametable writes.
                       Cheapest upgrade likely to reach 50 Hz in field play: a 68030 accelerator with fast RAM (not
                       measured).
Not done, not tested:  real A1200 hardware; NTSC machines; the sound by ear (checked as a WAV from tools/shost.c, not
                       listened to); interactive play by a person (1 player mode is translated from traces with random
                       input, but never played); the intro's split; a 68030 bench; CI (the ROM cannot be committed to
                       this public repository, so CI has nothing to build).
How to run:            put the ROM at original/bnb.nes, run tools/make_assets.sh, then tools/port.sh (or tools/build.sh
                       with the SOURCES line of port.conf). Copy dist/bnb to the Amiga and run "bnb" from a Shell
                       ("bnb bench 3000" for the proof run). Joystick in port 2: stick = pad, fire = A, second fire = B.
                       Keys: cursor keys = pad, X = A, Z or Alt = B, Space = Select, Return/Enter = Start, Esc = quit.
